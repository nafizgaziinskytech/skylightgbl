Our Approach

Organized Support for Service Operations

01

Define the Scope

Identify customer inquiry types, supported services, administrative tasks, and escalation responsibilities.

02

Document the Process

Establish procedures for account-related inquiries, complaints, service requests, and status communication.

03

Assign Responsibility

Define ownership, system access, escalation boundaries, and responsibilities for customer or technical decisions.

04

Review Execution

Review case handling, unresolved requests, documentation, and recurring service issues.

Responsible Operations

Clear Access. Controlled Information. Defined Escalation.

Telecom and utility workflows may involve personal information, customer account details, billing records, and service-related data.

Engagements should define identity verification requirements, authorized system access, permitted account changes, information handling, and escalation procedures.

Work affecting critical infrastructure, regulated activities, or technical service restoration must remain within the responsibilities and authorization of the appropriate operator.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What telecom and utility operations can SLG support?

Potential support includes customer care, billing inquiries, account administration, service request tracking, and back-office workflows.

Can support include complaint handling?

Teams can manage defined complaint workflows, record details, communicate status, and escalate cases according to the operator’s procedures.

Can outsourced teams access customer account systems?

Access must be explicitly authorized and configured according to the client’s security and operational requirements.

Can SLG support technical network operations?

The proposed page focuses on customer and business operations. Network engineering or infrastructure management should only be offered if those capabilities are separately verified.
