Responsible Operations

Financial Accuracy Starts With Defined Controls.

Financial administration can involve confidential records, payment-related information, payroll data, and transaction documentation.

Engagements should establish access permissions, segregation of responsibilities where appropriate, review procedures, approval thresholds, record retention requirements, and escalation routes.

Regulatory, tax, accounting, and statutory responsibilities must be clearly allocated between the client and service provider.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What finance and accounting tasks can SLG support?

Potential activities include bookkeeping support, invoice administration, accounts payable and receivable workflows, payroll administration, and financial record maintenance.

Can support be integrated with existing accounting systems?

System compatibility and access requirements can be assessed during scoping. Supported systems and responsibilities must be agreed upon before implementation.

Can outsourced teams initiate or approve payments?

Payment-related responsibilities must be explicitly defined. Initiation, authorization, and approval should remain subject to the client’s financial controls and delegated authority.

Can finance operations support scale with business growth?

Staffing and workflow arrangements can be evaluated against transaction volumes, reporting requirements, operating needs, and the agreed scope.
