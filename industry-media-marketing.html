Our Approach

Organized Execution Across Marketing Workflows

01

Define the Scope

Identify supported channels, campaign tasks, required systems, approval responsibilities, and expected deliverables.

02

Document the Process

Establish procedures for scheduling, content handling, lead management, campaign records, and escalation.

03

Assign Responsibility

Define task ownership, content approval, publishing permissions, CRM access, and responsibilities for client-facing communications.

04

Review Execution

Review task completion, record accuracy, campaign administration, and recurring workflow issues.

Responsible Operations

Consistent Execution. Controlled Publishing.

Marketing workflows may involve customer data, advertising accounts, campaign budgets, creative assets, and public-facing communications.

Engagements should establish account permissions, approval workflows, brand guidelines, data-handling requirements, publishing authority, and budget-related responsibilities.

Advertising claims, regulated content, and campaign decisions should be reviewed and approved by the appropriately authorized parties.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What marketing operations can SLG support?

Potential activities include campaign administration, lead operations, CRM maintenance, content coordination, social media support, and defined digital marketing tasks.

Does marketing operations support include creative strategy?

This page focuses on operational execution and coordination. Creative strategy should only be included if the relevant service is confirmed.

Can outsourced teams work with existing marketing tools?

Tool compatibility, permissions, and workflow requirements can be assessed during scoping.

Can support include paid-media administration?

It may be included where the relevant capability is available. Campaign budgets, account permissions, and approval responsibilities should be clearly defined.
