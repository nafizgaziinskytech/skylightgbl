Our Approach

Structured Support for Healthcare Workflows

01

Define the Scope

Identify the revenue cycle tasks, administrative workflows, systems, payers, and responsibilities included in the engagement.

02

Document the Process

Establish procedures for verification, claims, records, communication, and escalation.

03

Assign Responsibility

04

Review Execution

Review task completion, record accuracy, outstanding items, and reporting requirements.

Responsible Operations

Protected Information. Defined Responsibilities.

Healthcare workflows can involve protected health information, insurance details, billing records, and communication with patients, caregivers, and payers.

Engagements should define information access, privacy and security requirements, permitted communications, recordkeeping responsibilities, and escalation procedures.

Clinical decisions and other regulated healthcare activities must remain with appropriately licensed or authorized professionals.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What healthcare operations can SkyLight Global support?

Potential services include revenue cycle management, eligibility and authorization support, claims and payment workflows, healthcare administration, and patient and caregiver coordination.

How do SkyLight RCM and SkyHealth Pro differ?

SkyLight RCM focuses on revenue cycle management. SkyHealth Pro focuses on home care and healthcare operations. The right fit depends on the workflows your organization needs.

Can outsourced teams work in our EHR or billing systems?

System compatibility and authorized access can be assessed during scoping. Access should follow your organization’s privacy and security requirements.

Can healthcare support scale with our organization?

Staffing and workflow arrangements can be evaluated against volumes, operating hours, training needs, and the agreed scope.
