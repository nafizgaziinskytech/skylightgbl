SkyLight RCM

Specialized Support for Healthcare Revenue Cycle Operations

SkyLight RCM is the specialized brand within SkyLight Global’s service portfolio focused on healthcare revenue cycle management, supporting the administrative and financial workflows behind healthcare services.

Specialized focus

Focused on Healthcare Revenue Cycle Management

SkyLight RCM focuses on the financial and administrative processes that support healthcare reimbursement, from billing and claims workflows to payment follow-up and revenue-cycle administration.
