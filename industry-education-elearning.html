Responsible Operations

Protect Learner Information. Respect Institutional Responsibilities.

Education workflows may involve personal records, enrollment information, payment-related data, and information about minors.

Engagements should define access permissions, data-handling responsibilities, record retention, identity verification where appropriate, and escalation procedures.

Academic decisions, student assessments, and other institutionally controlled activities must remain with authorized educational personnel unless a specific and appropriate scope provides otherwise.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What education operations can SLG support?

Potential support includes learner inquiries, enrollment administration, record updates, scheduling coordination, and other defined administrative tasks.

Can support be provided for online learning platforms?

The scope can be assessed for learner support and administrative workflows associated with digital learning platforms, subject to system compatibility and service capability.

Can outsourced teams access student records?

Access must be authorized, limited to the agreed responsibilities, and managed according to the institution’s information-handling requirements.

Can support scale during enrollment periods?

Staffing and workflow arrangements can be assessed against enrollment volumes, seasonal demand, operating hours, and the agreed service scope.
