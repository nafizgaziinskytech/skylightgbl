Our journey

From a small team to a global delivery organization.

Founded in Dhaka in 2013, SkyTech began with a small team and a conviction that businesses could receive high-quality operational support from Bangladesh.

Over time, the organization expanded its workforce, entered new markets, developed additional capabilities, and invested in the infrastructure needed to support a broader range of business operations. Today, SkyTech Global brings together business process management, customer experience, and technology services within a growing delivery organization.

2013

Foundation & Humble Beginnings

SkyTech began operations in Dhaka with a small team and a commitment to delivering reliable business support.

2015

International Client Development

The organization expanded its operations, strengthened its support capabilities, and began serving international clients.

2017

Workforce Expansion

The growing operation passed 100 employees, marking an important step in its development.

2019

Industry Recognition & Memberships

SkyTech strengthened its industry participation through BACCO membership and the relevant telecommunications authorization.

2020

Operational Continuity

During the pandemic, the organization adapted its operating arrangements to support continued service delivery through changing working conditions.

2021

Execution & Market Recognition

SkyTech developed its operational capabilities and received recognition on industry platforms, including GoodFirms and Clutch.

2022

Acquisition of Technmart BPO

SkyTech expanded its operational footprint through the acquisition of Technmart BPO, a 100-seat call center.

2023

Corporate Transformation

The organization moved toward a more formal corporate structure and continued developing its operational and technology capabilities.

2024

Infrastructure & Workforce Growth

SkyTech relocated to Natore Tower and continued expanding its workforce and delivery capacity.

2025

International Expansion

The company expanded its international footprint through its U.S. incorporation and office establishment.

2026

Technology, AI & New Markets

SkyTech continued developing AI-enabled workflows and technology services while expanding its presence in Australia and the UAE.
